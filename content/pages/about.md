**The Daily Wire Demo** is an independent digital newsroom covering technology, business, science and culture. We launched in 2026 with a simple idea: readers deserve reporting that is fast, accurate and free from clutter.

## What we cover

Our four sections reflect the stories we believe matter most to a curious, globally minded reader:

- **Technology** — the products, platforms and policies shaping digital life.
- **Business** — markets, work and the economy, explained without jargon.
- **Science** — discoveries and research, reported with care for what is actually known.
- **Culture** — the films, music, games, books and ideas that move society.

## Our team

We are a small team of editors and reporters working remotely across three time zones. Every story carries the byline of the journalist who reported it, and every journalist is accountable for the accuracy of their work.

## How we are funded

The site is funded by advertising. Ads are clearly labelled and never influence editorial decisions. We do not accept payment for coverage, and we do not publish sponsored content disguised as news. Read our [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/) for details.

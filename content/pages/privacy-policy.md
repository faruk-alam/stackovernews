This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit this website, how we use it and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to the practices described here.

## Information we collect

This is a static website with no user accounts. We do not ask you to register or provide personal information to read our content.

### Automatically collected information

Our hosting provider records standard server logs, including your IP address, browser type, referring page and the pages you visit. These logs are used to keep the site secure and to understand aggregate traffic.

### Local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember a single preference: whether you have chosen light or dark mode. This value never leaves your device.

## Advertising

We display advertisements served by **Google AdSense**. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
- You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).
- You can also opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies by visiting [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

For visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, a consent message will be displayed before personalised advertising cookies are set, as required by law.

Articles may link to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to read their policies.

## Children

This site is not directed at children under the age of 16 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
